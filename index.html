Wish list rules

Your Secret Santa will read what you write, so make it fun to decode!

1

Keep it under ₹250 💸

The gift should not be worth more than ₹250. Think thoughtful, not expensive. Small, quirky and clever all win.

2

Don’t name the gift 🙈

No exact item, no brand, no product links. Your Santa should get to be a little creative and surprise you.

“A black leather wallet” → “Something sleek I’d carry everywhere, that makes me feel put together.”
3

Describe the vibe ✨

Be vague about the specifics. Tell us about a mood, a colour, a texture, a hobby or the moment you’d use it.

“A scented candle” → “Cosy rainy-evening energy, something that makes my room smell like a warm hug.”

Your wish 🎄

Remember: under ₹250, and no exact item names. Be mysterious 🕵️
